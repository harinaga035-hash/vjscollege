Our Vision & Mission

VJ's College of Pharmacy is a premier institution committed to imparting quality education in pharmaceutical sciences. Established with a vision to nurture competent professionals, the college provides a robust academic foundation integrated with real-world clinical and research exposure

Our Vision

To become a leader in the field of Pharmacy education where knowledge is created transferred and internalised value systems are imbedded in the creation of responsible citizens and to participate in nation building.

Our Mission

Create, nurture and grow an organisation of inspired, innovative & knowledge driven educators, who will in turn mould top class ethical Pharmacy professionals market driven and market oriented learning, delivery and implementation method



Our Management

Dr. N.V.V. Jagan Mohan Reddy, M.Pharm, PhD

Secretary & Correspondent

Dr.N.V.V.Jagan Mohan Reddy is the Founder, Secretary& Correspondent of VJ’s College of Pharmacy, Diwancheruvu, Rajahmundry, Andhra Pradesh. He completed his Bachelor of Pharmacy at Bangalore University. He has completed M.Pharmacy & Ph.D at Andhra University, Visakhapatnam.

He worked at Various Pharma Industries. He is having Numerous Research Articles in National & International Journals. He is Self-Confident always believed in his honesty and his hard work. He believe Education plays an important role in life, Education helps a person to get knowledge and improve confidence in life. It can help to improve career & personal growth. An educated person can become a great citizen in Society. He is having an educational leadership Qualities. He is Well experience in Academics & Administration. He is a most Passionate about Pharmacy. To impart Quality education he started his Own Pharmacy College in the year 2007. He having a strong Vision of the college is to emerge as one of the top Pharmacy College in the Country and Provide Pharmacy Professional of Global Standards. He always thought a head of his time and want to prepare the young Talented People to meet the challenges of future. He always want to provide strategic plan in the development of academic System in Pharmacy education. He also to asses teaching methods. Monitor student achievement & revise policies and Procedures.
