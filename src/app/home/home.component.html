About VJ's College of Pharmacy

VJ’s College of Pharmacy is among the leading colleges of Andhra Pradesh, established in 2007 in Rajahmundry. With the help of a highly experienced and passionate team of Teaching Faculty with good educational practices, we strive to achieve excellence in education and knowledge. We aim to promote this excellence in the field of Pharmacy and prepare upcoming generations to face challenges in various Pharmaceutical industries like research, clinical, hospital practice, education, and development.

At UG Level - B.Pharmacy - Bachelor of Pharmacy (100 Seats) is a Four-year undergraduate program focused on the study of drugs, medications, and their effects on the human body. It's a popular choice for science students after 12th grade (M.P.C / Bi.P. C), offering a broad curriculum encompassing pharmaceutical sciences, medicinal chemistry, pharmacology, and more. Graduates are equipped for careers in the pharmaceutical industry, healthcare, and related fields.

Pharm.D (30 Seats) at UG Level is a six-year professional pharmacy program offered at our college, designed to prepare students for clinical and hospital-based pharmacy practice. It integrates pharmaceutical science with patient care to develop competent healthcare professionals.

At PG Level- Master of Pharmacy (M.Pharm) program is a postgraduate degree focused on advanced pharmaceutical sciences, typically taking two years to complete. It provides specialized knowledge in areas like drug development, formulation, and usage. Students develop skills in research, analysis, and practical application within the pharmaceutical industry or related fields. M.Pharmacy in Pharmaceutics (15 seats), Pharmaceutical Analysis (15 seats) and Pharmacology (15 seats) courses with all necessary approvals from AICTE - New Delhi, PCI - New Delhi, AP State Government and is affiliated to Andhra University.
